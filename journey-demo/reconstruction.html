About this reconstruction & downloads

The people, colors and natural scenery come from the original travel footage. Estimated depth turns each frame into a 3D surface, and the recorded movement plays across it. Explore nearby viewpoints for the best result. Wide rotations reveal the limits of the source view: hidden sides are not modeled, and people are not separate rigged characters.

Geometry was estimated locally on DGX Spark with MoGe-2, then assembled and rendered in NVIDIA Omniverse. This generation made no StepFun API calls. The interactive browser uses the same geometry and original video textures.
